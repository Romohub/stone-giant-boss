# Stone Giant

A 5-minute final boss fight in Decentraland, built for GAME OVER 2026: The Final Boss Fight.

Play it here: https://decentraland.org/jump?realm=rgl6.dcl.eth

## How to play

- Wait for the giant's eye to open, then click it. Clicks only count while the eye is open.
- Land 3 hits to win.
- You have 3 HP and 5 minutes. Falling rocks cost 1 HP when they land next to you.

## Phases

- Phase 1: the eye opens every 10 seconds.
- Phase 2: the eye opens every 6 seconds and rocks start falling.
- Phase 3: the eye opens every 4 seconds, rocks fall faster, and the arena walls turn red.

Win and lose screens both offer a PLAY AGAIN button that resets the fight.
